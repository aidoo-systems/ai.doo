Questions

Who am I dealing with?

Keith, who runs ai.doo on the Isle of Man and builds the games. Email or WhatsApp, and it's him who answers.

How do I pay, and when?

Nothing until you've said yes to a game idea. Then a £300 deposit by bank transfer starts the build, and the rest is due when your game goes live.

The deposit pays for the build, so it isn't refunded once work starts. If you play your game before launch and decide not to go live, you owe nothing more.

How does a winner get the prize?

They show the winner's screen on their phone at the counter. It has a moving clock, so it can't be a screenshot, and your staff tap it once when the prize is handed over.

The demo has an example: finish a game and tap “See the winner's screen”. In your game, only the real winner would get it.

How will I know anyone played?

We'll send you the number of plays each week. Players stay anonymous: a nickname at most, no emails, no personal data.

What makes it more than £750?

£750 is one of our simple one-tap games, themed to you, for one location. A brand-new game idea, separate boards for several locations or a longer run costs more. You get the price in writing before you pay anything.
