How it works

01

A 20-minute chat

You tell us about your business or event and who you want to reach. We suggest a simple game idea that fits, and you say yes or no.

02

About two weeks to build

Your logo, colours and products, in a one-tap game anyone can pick up in seconds. You play it before it goes live and ask for changes.

03

Three months live

You get a link and QR code artwork for print. A leaderboard gives players a reason to come back. Then it switches off, unless you want to keep it running.

Good for

  • A café or shop: “top score this week wins a free coffee”, with a QR code at the till.
  • A restaurant or takeaway: something to play between ordering and eating, with the code on the menu or the table.
  • A pub or bar: a weekly top score for the regulars, played in the queue at the bar.
  • Race weeks and festivals: something for visitors to play in the queue or at your stand.
  • A charity fundraiser: a leaderboard people share with friends.
  • A launch or anniversary: something people actually play, rather than another leaflet.

If you offer a prize, it's yours to run; we keep the game one of skill.

The kind of game we mean

Tea Tower is our one-tap stacker: a teacup swings above a growing tower, and you tap to drop it. Easy to learn, hard to put down. A promo game is the same idea, built around your brand. Try it on Google Play, or ask and we'll show it to you running on a phone.
