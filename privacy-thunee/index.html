Privacy Policy — Thunee

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Thunee is a card game by ai.doo (Aidoo Systems), built in Godot. The game is designed to run entirely on your device. This policy explains what the game stores locally and confirms that no game data is sent to us or to anyone else. Our website: https://www.aidoo.biz.

Data we collect

None. Thunee does not collect or transmit personal information or other user data. It does not create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

Information stored on your device

Thunee stores a small configuration file in the app's private local storage so that it can remember your preferences and resume an unfinished match. It contains:

  • Audio volume and mute settings
  • Your selected table theme
  • The current match score, dealer position, and number of hands played
  • The computer players selected for that match and their in-game personalities

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded, shared, used for advertising, or associated with an online identity.

Network access and permissions

Thunee makes no network connections and the Android build does not request internet access or any other Android permissions. The game has no online multiplayer, leaderboards, cloud saves, or user accounts.

Advertising, analytics, and third-party services

Thunee contains no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, or tracking technology. It does not share data with third parties.

The platform from which you obtain the game, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store, may process information under its own privacy policy. That platform activity occurs outside Thunee and is not data collected by the game.

Data retention and deletion

Preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. A saved match is removed when the match ends or when you choose to abandon it. To remove all local Thunee data, clear the app's storage using your device or operating-system settings. On platforms where uninstalling removes app storage, uninstalling the game will do the same. Because no game data is sent to us, there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete on our servers.

Data security

Thunee keeps its settings and saved-match information in the app's private local storage and does not transmit it over a network. Access to that storage is controlled by your device and operating system.

Children's privacy

Thunee is a general-audience card game and is not directed at children under 13. The game collects no personal information and includes no accounts, chat, advertising, or other online interaction.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and the relevant store disclosures before that version is released. The date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.
