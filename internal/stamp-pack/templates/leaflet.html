Your stamp card

$Stamps_word stamps earns $reward_lower. It works like your paper card, but it lives on the customer's phone.

1

Getting a card

The customer points their phone camera at the QR sign on the counter and taps Get my card. It goes into Apple or Google Wallet. There's nothing to download and nothing to sign up for.

2

Stamping

Open the stamper on the shop phone, scan the card on the customer's phone and tap +1. The stamp shows up on their card straight away.

3

The free $reward_noun

On the $stamps_ordinal stamp, the stamper shows Give free item. Tap it, give them their $reward_noun, and their card starts again.

Day to day

  • For a bigger order, you can add more than one stamp in one go.
  • Pressed the wrong thing? Undo takes the stamp off again. You've got 30 seconds.
  • If the signal drops, keep stamping as normal. The phone saves the stamps and sends them once it's connected again.
  • Shop shows how many stamps you've given today and in total.
  • Customers can't stamp their own cards. Only your shop phone can.

Worth knowing

  • The QR on your sign never changes, so you can put it on menus or post it on Facebook and Instagram.

Setting up the shop phone

On an iPhone

Open the stamper in Safari and leave that tab open. Don't add it to the Home Screen: it won't be paired there and can't stamp.

On an Android phone

Open the stamper in Chrome, tap the menu (the three dots) and choose Add to Home screen. Then open it from the new icon.
