Talking points, in order

1. Hello, and why you've come in

"Hi, I'm Keith from ai.doo. I messaged you about a stamp card for your customers' phones. I've made one up in your colours: can I show you? It takes two minutes."

  • If it's busy, ask when's quiet and come back. Don't pitch over a queue
  • Do they have a paper card now? How many stamps, and what do people get?
  • Who comes back most: regulars, office workers, visitors?

2. The card, live

  • Put the framed sign on the counter: "This one's already set up for you."
  • Ask them to scan it with their own phone, tap Get my card and add it to their Wallet
  • Stamp it from your paired iPhone and let them watch the stamp appear

3. How it would run

  • Their own phone at the counter does the stamping. Pair it now if they say yes
  • Is the number of stamps and the reward right? Easy to change
  • Staff side: +1, Undo for mistakes, and it keeps working with no signal
  • No customer details are kept. Customers can't stamp their own card
  • It runs alongside their paper cards: nothing changes at the till

4. The price

  • Two weeks free to try it
  • Then £15 a month for the whole business
  • No setup fee, no contract, stop whenever they like

5. Close

  • Agree one next step: start the two weeks today, or a day that suits
  • If yes: pair their phone, leave the sign and the leaflet, and send the details with Share with the shop
  • If not now: thank them, and take the frame home with you
Remember: never send a pairing link in a message; pair in person or over a video call. Don't promise anything that isn't built: "that's something we could make" is fine. Don't name the other shop on the card until they've said yes. Their logo is for their pack only, never in a post without their yes.
