How it works

  1. A small sign by the till. Customers scan it and tap Add to Wallet, once.
  2. Each visit, staff scan the card with the shop's own phone. One stamp per item.
  3. The card updates on the customer's phone a moment later, with a note on the lock screen.
  4. When it's full, the till says so. One tap gives the free item and the card starts again.

What it isn't

  • No customer data. No names, emails or phone numbers, so nothing to protect or leak.
  • No marketing. No messages or offers sent to your customers.
  • No card machine changes. It sits beside your till, not in it.
  • No self-stamping. A stamp always needs your staff's phone.

This is a demo. Nothing on this page is stored or sent, and the cards here are pretend.
