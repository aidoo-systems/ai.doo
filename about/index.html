About

Hi, I'm Keith. I make small games.

ai.doo is a small family studio on the Isle of Man. I make the games and apps; my wife Bineeta and our son Luke are my first testers, and the toughest. Now I make games for local businesses too: a short one with your name on it, for your customers to play while they wait.

  • A family studio, so you deal with me
  • Made here, on the Isle of Man
  • Games out on Google Play

Why games

I like things you can play in the gaps: waiting for a coffee, a bus, the kids. A minute that leaves you smiling is a good minute, and most of what I build starts as something I wanted on my own phone.

Everything gets played at our kitchen table first. If Luke puts it down after a minute, it goes back in the oven.

I care about the small stuff: that it opens fast, feels good under your thumb, and doesn't ask for your email or fill your screen with ads.

Why ai.doo?

It's my surname, Naidoo, without the N. It started when I was building AI tools, and the name stuck after the games took over.

Why make them for businesses

A café or a pub already has people waiting. A short game with your name on it gives them something to do, a reason to come back and beat their score, and a prize you choose for the top of the board. I build it, keep it running, and you get one person to talk to.
