# Stamps outreach, batch 3: The Roundhouse and Gourmet Shakes (P2.0, D-010)

Status: **draft, 2026-11-04**, for the owner to adjust and send himself. Same rules as
[batch 2](2026-11-03-stamps-outreach-batch-2.md): plain text from your own address or Facebook
profile, no family names, one follow-up after a week, then stop. Log each one in the
[prospect list](2026-10-02-promo-prospects.md)'s contact log when sent, with its channel.

The offer is D-037's, as in batch 2: **a free two-week pilot alongside their paper cards, then
£15 a month, no setup fee, no contract, and the first shops keep that price.** Still no start
date until the owner says so.

## Why these two

Both found by the owner. **The Roundhouse** is the only Island business seen announcing a new
paper stamp card this year: its soft play started one on 28 July 2026. **Gourmet Shakes** is a
shake café in central Douglas, the kind of counter a card is for.

Both links carry **the shop's own logo**, from their own site (`&logo=`). It's for their private
sample only: not in any post or video unless they say yes.

## Before sending

As before: open their link on your phone first; shop and reward take letters, digits, spaces
and `& ' . -` only; stamps 4 to 12. The Roundhouse's card is confirmed by their own post;
Gourmet Shakes' reward is a guess, so swap in what they'd give.
