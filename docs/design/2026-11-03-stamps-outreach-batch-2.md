# Stamps outreach, batch 2: Onchan's Village Walk and The Caff (P2.0, D-010)

Status: **draft, 2026-11-03**, for the owner to adjust and send himself. Same rules as
[the second outreach emails](2026-10-22-second-outreach-emails.md): plain text from your own
address or Facebook profile, no family names, one follow-up after a week, then stop. Log each
one in the [prospect list](2026-10-02-promo-prospects.md)'s contact log when sent, with its
channel.

What's changed since S1 to S4: the price. **A free two-week pilot alongside their paper cards,
then £15 a month, no setup fee, no contract, and the first shops keep that price** (D-037; the
`/stamps/` page says the same since 2026-11-02). Both wallets are built now (loyalty P1.5 and
P1.6 Complete), but still **no start date** until the owner says so.

Where the batch-1 shops stand (2026-11-03): Gelatory had its one follow-up on 2026-10-30 and
Roots on 2026-11-02, both without an answer, so both stop unless they write. Porter's Kitchen
and Drewry's had their first email on 2026-10-23; no follow-up is logged for them.

## Why these five

Four of them sit on or beside **Village Walk, Onchan** (IM3 4EA): a café, two dog groomers
and a barber within a couple of minutes' walk, so one morning on foot covers them, phone in
hand. The fifth, **The Caff** on Lord Street, Douglas, is the only Island shop the web shows
with a paper stamp card (8 stamps, a free meal), and it's ten minutes' drive away.

Checked on the web on 2026-11-03. **Only The Caff's card is confirmed, and only by an undated
Tripadvisor review.** The rest are counters where a paper card is likely (a groomer every six
to eight weeks, a barber every few) but unseen: look at the counter first, and if there's a
card, put its count and reward in the link before you show it.

**One thing to ask about: Loyal IOM.** A local app (Max Oyston, on both app stores) does stamps
by tapping an NFC tag at the till, at "participating cafes, restaurants and salons", and The
Sound Cafe is one. If a shop already uses it, log it in the "We already use X" column and
don't push: that's the experiment's data, not an objection to beat.

**Two groomers on one walk.** Madrina and The Barking Barber are next door to each other.
Pitch both, but don't mention one to the other, and the pilot is one shop at a time:
whoever says yes first.

Spares on the same walk, if one has closed or says no: **Scissors Hairdressers** (next door to
the Nutty Chef) and **Heavenly Hounds** (groomer, Unit 3C Village Walk).

and `& ' . -` only; stamps 4 to 12; colours are left off below (the demo's own), so add a hex
