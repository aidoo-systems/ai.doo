## Ice cream emails (first contact)

The demo pours soft serve now: `?skin=icecream` is the Giddy Puffin kiosk, and each round names
its serve (a 99 with a flake, a cup with sauce, a strawberry twist with sprinkles, a quicker
99, a twist in a cup). Both prospects are seasonal, so these pitch **next season**: most of the
Island's ice cream counters are quiet or shut until Easter, and a deposit before the
2026-11-26 read is unlikely (P2.0u's note). That's fine: the point is a conversation now.

Before sending:

- **Check they serve soft serve.** The demo pours a whippy. If the counter only scoops, say
  "a sample of the feel" if asked, as with the panto; don't promise a scoop game (that's a
  new skin, and not built).
- Open the link on your phone first: their name should be on the title and the board.
- `?for=icecream` on the offer link is the kind of venue, never the business (D-017).
