What changed on the evening of 2026-10-20, at the owner's word:

- No family names in the emails. "A small family studio" stays; the About page link in the
  signature is there for anyone who wants more. (The About page itself still names them:
  the owner's call.)

  business gets, and what it costs them in effort. No claim of results we haven't seen.
- One line on the risk: they play their game before it goes live, and if they don't want it,
  they owe nothing beyond the £300 deposit (as the offer page says).
- A sixth email, the Isle of Man Creamery, now that the demo pours milk (P2.0s, D-027).

Before sending each one:

- Find a named person and the right address (website, Facebook page, or ask at the counter).
  Replace `<name>`; if there is no name, "Hi there" is fine.
- Open the demo link on your phone first: their name should be on the title and the board.
- Send it from your own address, not a mailing tool, as plain text. One follow-up after a
  week, then stop.
- The name is in the link only; the game stores and sends nothing (D-017). The `?for=` on the
  offer link is the kind of venue, never the business.
- The prize is the business's to set and check (D-025); we don't advise on promotion law.

November), then the two cafés (they decide in a week), then the Creamery, then Okell's and
Hospice (slower, bigger, a winter conversation either way).
