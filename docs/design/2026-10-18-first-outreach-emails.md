# First five outreach emails (P2.0, D-010)

Status: **draft, rewritten 2026-10-20**, for the owner to adjust and send himself. From the
[prospect list](2026-10-02-promo-prospects.md); log each one in its contact log when sent.

Checked on the web on 2026-10-18: all five are trading. Dropped from the first five:
**The Tea Junction** (#22), whose owners announced in 2025 that they were closing at the end
of their lease and handing over to new operators; check who runs it now before writing.

What changed on 2026-10-20: each demo link opens on the right venue (`?skin=cafe` or
`?skin=pub`, P2.0q) with the business's own name on it (`?name=`, P2.0l), at the game's new
address, `/play/pour/` (P2.0r). Each email now reads as what we are: one person and his family,
on the Island, with the About page (`/about/`) for anyone who wants to see who Keith is.

Before sending each one:

- Find a named person and the right address (website, Facebook page, or ask at the counter).
  Replace `<name>`; if there is no name, "Hi there" is fine.
- Open the demo link on your phone first: their name should be on the title and the board.
- Send it from your own address, not a mailing tool. One follow-up after a week, then stop.
- The name is in the link only; the game stores and sends nothing (D-017). The `?for=` on the
  offer link is the kind of venue, never the business.
- The prize is the business's to set and check (D-025); we don't advise on promotion law.

Order: the two cafés first (they decide in a week), then the panto (it has a deadline), then
Okell's and Hospice (slower, bigger, a winter conversation either way).
