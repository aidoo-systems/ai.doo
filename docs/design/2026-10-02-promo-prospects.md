# Promo games: starter prospect list (P2.0, D-010)

Status: **draft, 2026-10-02**. Organisations only, from public listings; no individuals.
Before contacting anyone, check that each one still exists and find its contact route (website, Facebook page, or walk in).
Aim for 40–60 contacts by 2026-11-26. Log every contact in the table at the end. Pass bar: one deposit of £300 or more.

**Timing matters.** Organisations with a season (TT is late May to early June; the Festival of Motorcycling is late August) plan and budget months ahead. A game for TT 2027 is a winter conversation, so this experiment is the right moment for them. Cafés and shops can decide in a week.

## The message (send it yourself, adjust per prospect)

> Hi <name>, I'm Keith from ai.doo, a small games studio on the Isle of Man. We make short branded games that people play on their phone straight from a QR code, with no app to download. For <business/event>, I'm thinking of <one-line idea, e.g. "a pour-the-perfect-latte game with a weekly board; top score wins coffee and cake">. It's from £750, live in about two weeks, and it runs for three months. Here's one you can play in half a minute: <demo link>. How it works and the price: <offer link>. Would a 20-minute chat be useful?

Open with a specific idea for them, not "we do games". One follow-up after a week, then stop.

Links to paste (added 2026-10-11; moved to `/play/pour/` 2026-10-20, P2.0r). Send the skin nearest their trade, and tag both links with the kind of venue, never the business's name, so Umami shows which kind responds:

| For | Demo link | Offer link |
|---|---|---|
| A café, shop or restaurant | `aidoo.biz/play/pour/?skin=cafe` | `aidoo.biz/promo-games/?for=cafe` |
| A pub or bar | `aidoo.biz/play/pour/?skin=pub` | `aidoo.biz/promo-games/?for=pub` |
| An event, charity or attraction | `aidoo.biz/play/pour/?skin=pub` | `aidoo.biz/promo-games/?for=event` |

The game itself is not tracked ("Nothing is stored or sent" is printed on it), so a demo link shows nothing in Umami until they come back to the offer page, which arrives as `?from=demo`. The offer page's buttons report `play-demo-hero`, `play-demo-pub`, `play-demo-cafe`, `email-nav`, `email-contact` and `whatsapp-contact`.
