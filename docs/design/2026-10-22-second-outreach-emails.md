# Second outreach emails: stamp cards and more pour games (P2.0, D-010)

Status: **draft, 2026-10-22**, for the owner to adjust and send himself. Same rules as
[the first emails](2026-10-18-first-outreach-emails.md): plain text from your own address,
no family names, one follow-up after a week, then stop. Log each one in the
[prospect list](2026-10-02-promo-prospects.md)'s contact log when sent.

Two pitches, and a business gets one of them, never both:

- **Stamp cards** (`aidoo.biz/stamps/`, the loyalty repo's product) for shops whose customers
  come back every week or month. The product isn't built yet (no wallet passes or server;
  loyalty's P1.2 is next), so the offer is the owner's call of 2026-10-22: **a free early
  pilot for the first few Island shops, alongside their paper cards. No price yet**: that
  waits for the Gelatory pilot (loyalty's A6). Don't promise a start date.
- **Pour games** (`aidoo.biz/play/pour/`, from £750) for places with a wait or a crowd.

Checked on the web on 2026-10-22: each one below has a listing or news from 2025 or 2026,
but none confirms a paper stamp card or a named contact. Look at the counter first.

## Before sending a stamps email

- **Open their link on your phone first.** Their name, icon, reward and stamp count come
  from the link; anything the page can't use falls back to the plain demo. The reward and
  shop name take letters, digits, spaces and `& ' . -` only (no commas). Icons:
  coffee, tea, icecream, cake, croissant, donut, cookie, sandwich, pizza, burger, flower,
  scissors, paw, star. Stamps: 4 to 12. Colour: a hex from their sign or website, without
  the `#`. The colours below are placeholders except Gelatory's.
- If they already have a paper card, copy its count and reward into the link. That's the
  strongest thing the email can show.
- The free pilot runs alongside the paper card, so nothing changes for customers who don't
  want it. Two weeks, at one shop.
- Don't claim what isn't built: no dates, no "live", no numbers from other shops.

## Order

Gelatory first (it's the pilot the loyalty repo is built around). Then Porter's Kitchen and
Drewry's (repeat customers, two sites each), then the Peel cafés. Pour games alongside:
Douglas City Council has a December deadline, so it goes this week.
