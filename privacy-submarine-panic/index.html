Privacy Policy — Submarine Panic

Last updated: September 2026

Submarine Panic is a mobile game built in Godot. The whole game plays offline on your device. It has one online feature, an optional fleet board, and you can switch it off. This policy explains what the game keeps on your phone, what the board sends if you leave it on, and how to have an entry removed. Our website: https://www.aidoo.biz.

Data we collect

Submarine Panic does not create an account and never asks who you are. Everything the game needs lives in a save file on your phone. The only thing that leaves your device is a fleet board entry, and only while posting is switched on.

The game does keep a small save file on your device so it can remember your progress between sessions. That file stores:

  • Your settings, such as volume, haptics, and the CRT visual toggle
  • Your high score and current rank
  • Records of your past patrols (the in-game incident summaries)
  • Your fleet board callsign, and whether posting is switched on

This information stays in local storage on your device. None of it is transmitted except the handful of fields listed under “The fleet board” below.

Network and online features

Every part of the game plays offline. The one network feature is the fleet board, described below. The app requests the internet permission so the board can work, and nothing else in the game uses it. There are no online accounts and no cloud saves, so your progress is never uploaded and never restored from anywhere.

The fleet board

The fleet board ranks finished patrols against other players, with a board for the day’s dive and an all-time board. When a patrol ends and posting is switched on, the game sends these fields, and nothing else, over HTTPS to our own service at spsb.aidoo.biz:

  • A random player ID generated on your device the first time you open the board
  • Your callsign, which the game suggests and you can change
  • Your score, how long the patrol lasted, how deep you went, and how many hunters you sank
  • Which mode you played, and for a daily dive, the date and the condition it ran on
  • The version of the game and the time the patrol finished

That is the whole list. There is no real name, no email address, no location, no device or advertising identifier, and nothing that identifies your phone. The player ID is random and is not linked to an account, because there are no accounts.

Switching it off. Open the fleet board from the title screen and press POSTING. With posting off, no patrol is ever sent, the board does not ask the server who you are, and any patrols saved while you had no signal are discarded rather than sent later. The rest of the game is unchanged, and you can still read the board.

If you play with no signal. A finished patrol waits on your device and is sent the next time the game can reach the service, so an entry may arrive a day or more after you played. Switching posting off clears anything still waiting.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or payment data. There are no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the game. The one thing that is public is the board itself. Your callsign, score and rank are visible to other players, which is the point of a leaderboard. Your player ID is never shown to anyone.

We may change or remove a callsign that is offensive or abusive. This does not affect your score.

Storage and deletion

Your save file is stored on your device. To remove it, uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings. Clearing the data also discards your player ID, so any board entries you posted are left with no way to trace them back to you.

To have a board entry removed, or to report a callsign, email hello@aidoo.biz and include the callsign shown in the game. We will remove the entry.

Children's privacy

Submarine Panic is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The game asks for no personal information at any point. The fleet board carries only a random ID, a callsign and the numbers from a patrol, and it can be switched off entirely.

Changes to this policy

If a change to the game changes how data is handled, we will update this policy before that change ships, as we did when the fleet board replaced the offline-only version described in the previous policy.
