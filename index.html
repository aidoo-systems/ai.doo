How it works

How a promo game gets made.

From a first chat to a game people play from a QR code.

01

Concept chat

Twenty minutes about your business or event, and who you want to reach.

02

The idea

We suggest a simple game that fits. You say yes or no.

03

Build

About two weeks. Your logo, colours and products, in a one-tap game anyone can pick up.

04

You try it before anyone else does, and get one round of changes.

A link and QR code artwork for the till, your stand or a poster. Plays in any phone browser.
