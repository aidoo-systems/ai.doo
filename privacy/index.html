Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 April 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how ai.doo ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at aidoo.biz, our mobile applications available on the Google Play Store, and our products (including PIKA and VERA). We are trading as ai.doo and are subject to the Isle of Man Data Protection Act 2018 (which provides protections equivalent to the UK GDPR and EU GDPR).

We have designed our website and products with privacy in mind. We do not run advertising trackers or sign-up forms that collect personal data. We use a single, privacy-respecting, self-hosted analytics platform (described in section 2) to understand which pages are visited.

2. Information we collect

Our web server automatically records standard access logs when you visit aidoo.biz. These logs may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser user agent string (browser type and version)
  • Pages requested and timestamps
  • HTTP status codes and response sizes

We use Umami, a privacy-respecting, self-hosted analytics platform, to understand which pages are visited and how visitors arrive. Umami runs on our own infrastructure at analytics.aidoo.biz. It does not use cookies, does not track visitors across sites, and does not collect personal data. IP addresses are used only to derive an approximate country and are then discarded; no individual visitor profile is stored. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, or any third-party data processor for analytics.

3. Fonts

This website self-hosts its font files (Inter). No requests are made to Google Fonts or any other external font provider. There are no third-party data touch points on this site beyond the chat widget described below.

4. Our products (PIKA and VERA)

PIKA and VERA are self-hosted applications designed to run entirely within your own infrastructure. Data you enter — including documents, queries, and OCR results — is processed on your own systems. ai.doo does not receive, store, or have access to that data.

  • PIKA — document intelligence and Q&A via retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
  • VERA — OCR validation platform for scanned documents, receipts, and invoices

Both products use local AI models via Ollama. No data is sent to external AI services unless you explicitly configure a cloud endpoint.

4a. Chat widget

The chat widget on aidoo.biz uses OpenAI's API (gpt-4o-mini model) to answer questions about our products. When you send a message through the chat widget:

  • Your message text is sent to OpenAI's servers for processing
  • Your IP address is logged by our server for rate limiting
  • Your conversation history within the current browser session is sent alongside each new message to provide context — it is not stored on our servers and is cleared when you close the tab

OpenAI's data handling is governed by their privacy policy. The chat widget is only used on the marketing site — it is completely separate from the self-hosted PIKA and VERA products.

5. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies. No tracking cookies, session cookies, or third-party cookies are used.

6. How we use information

Server access logs are used solely for security monitoring and troubleshooting purposes. We do not sell, share, or otherwise disclose log data to third parties, except where required by law.

7. Data retention

Server access logs are retained for approximately 90 days, after which they are automatically deleted.

8. Your rights

Under the Isle of Man Data Protection Act 2018 you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with the Isle of Man Information Commissioner (ico.org.im)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@aidoo.biz.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please email hello@aidoo.biz.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of our website or products after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Data deletion

PIKA and VERA use centralised user accounts managed through the ai.doo Hub. An administrator can disable or delete user accounts from the Hub admin panel. To request deletion of your account or associated data, contact your organisation's administrator or email hello@aidoo.biz.
